## Couple photographs

No couple photographs were supplied with this project. The photograph slots in `public/photos/` are intentionally empty and show gold frames with an “Add photo” control. They were **not** filled with stock images or unrelated people. Add the couple's own photographs using the file names documented in `README.md`, or upload them in the Photo Studio.
